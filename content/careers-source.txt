Who we are

Hopkins MedTech Compliance is an independent contract research organization (CRO) for medical devices and in vitro diagnostics (IVD). Operated by Hopkins MedTech Group LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company, we plan and run clinical performance studies and prepare the regulatory submissions that follow, for the United States (FDA) and the European Union.

The work

Our teams cover four areas.

• Clinical operations: study documents, site coordination, enrollment and visit tracking.

• Regulatory affairs: submission pathways, requirements and the evidence behind FDA and EU filings.

• Clinical data and biostatistics: data review and performance measures such as sensitivity, specificity and agreement.

• Quality and compliance: procedures, audit evidence and document control.

We also run an early-stage research initiative in AI and digital health, including facial and skin image analysis.


How we work with early career colleagues

Each person works with a named project lead, meets on a set weekly cadence, and receives written feedback on every deliverable. Work is evaluated on accuracy and traceability of sources, documentation quality, responsiveness to feedback, and judgment about what to escalate. Everyone works only with data and documents they are authorized to access. Confidential and patient-related information is never uploaded to public or unapproved AI tools, and all AI-assisted work is verified before it enters a deliverable.
