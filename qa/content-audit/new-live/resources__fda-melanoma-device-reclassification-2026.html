FDA AND REGULATORY

FDA reclassification of two melanoma assessment device types

Hopkins MedTech Compliance · 2026
Source publisher: Hopkins MedTech Compliance
Original LinkedIn post: 2026-10-05 · Editorial summary with primary-source references
HMC / PERSPECTIVES
FDA and Regulatory
Regulatory context checked on 8 October 2026. Reclassification of a device type does not clear or approve an individual product.

A 2026 final order changes the pathway for specified adjunctive devices while retaining special controls.

What changed

FDA’s final order, published on 25 March 2026 and effective 24 April 2026, moved optical diagnostic devices for melanoma and electrical impedance spectrometers from class III to class II with special controls. The order identifies device types associated with product codes OYD and ONV and subjects them to premarket notification.

The scope matters

These are adjunctive devices used in clinical assessment. The order does not create a general pathway for every skin-analysis app, consumer imaging tool or autonomous diagnostic system. Its clinical-performance and labeling controls must be read with each device definition and intended use.

From classification to an evidence plan

The practical question is whether a proposed product fits a specified classification, then what evidence supports that intended use. Our editorial suggestion is to document the target users, patients, output and role in the care pathway before translating a classification decision into study requirements. A different intended use may require a different assessment.
