How AI is used in FDA-authorized medical devices: a taxonomy across 1,016 authorizations

NPJ digital medicine · 2025
Paper authors: Singh R, Bapna M, Diab AR, Ruiz ES, Lotter W.
Research reading note · Based on the abstract · Editorial summary prepared for this HMC website preview
HMC / PERSPECTIVES
Digital Health

A taxonomy of authorized AI devices helps separate clinical purpose, AI function and input data.

What the paper explores

The authors reviewed 1,016 FDA authorizations to classify how AI is used in medical devices. The taxonomy describes differences in clinical tasks and technical functions, including quantitative image analysis and data generation. It provides a more specific vocabulary than treating all medical AI as one category.

How to interpret the evidence

The counts and technology observations describe the study’s historical dataset. They are not a live inventory of FDA authorizations or proof of the performance of any individual device.

Questions for MedTech teams

Describe the product’s actual function, inputs, outputs and role in care before designing its evaluation. Different AI functions call for different evidence questions.
